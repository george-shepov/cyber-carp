Why laser cleaning

Less mess between the contamination and the material.

Laser cleaning uses a precise, controllable beam to remove selected surface contamination or coatings. It can be a strong fit for many restoration and preparation jobs—but every substrate, coating, condition, and desired finish is different.

Questions before the first pass

Useful answers.
No overpromises.

Can every material be laser cleaned?

No. Suitability depends on the substrate, coating, contamination, condition, geometry, equipment settings, and desired result. Every project is evaluated before work begins.

Is laser cleaning dust-free or damage-free?

Laser cleaning is precise and can reduce abrasive-media waste in suitable applications, but it is not universally risk-free or damage-proof. Appropriate controls, PPE, and operator judgment remain essential.

Do you provide a price automatically?

No. The inquiry form gathers enough information for a useful review. Final pricing depends on the material, scope, access, travel, and desired finish.

Do you offer appointments?

For the MVP, customers submit preferred timing and Den confirms manually. Live availability can be added after the service area, working hours, and appointment types are confirmed.

Can I send photos from my phone?

Yes. The estimate form accepts multiple images and short videos. Do not upload private information that is unrelated to the project.
