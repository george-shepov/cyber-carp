# Cyber Carp launch content checklist

## Den must confirm

- Full name and preferred owner/operator bio
- Public phone number, email, hours, and response expectations
- Final service radius and travel-fee policy, if any
- Training provider and wording Den is comfortable publishing
- Equipment make/model, laser type, and power rating
- Insurance status and any licenses or registrations that may be represented
- Accepted payment methods and whether deposits are required
- Privacy policy, service-request terms, upload retention period, and consent wording

## Shoot plan

For at least three objects, capture the full untreated object, close-ups, material identification, Den’s explanation, machine setup, PPE/safety-zone preparation, first pass, half-cleaned comparison, finished result, treatment time, and a final explanation.

Deliver: one short muted hero loop, three to six horizontal project videos, three vertical social clips, one operator introduction, and one equipment/safety clip. Keep the original files outside the website repository; publish optimized derivatives through object storage/CDN.

## Before launch QA

- Test form submission, file limits, signed uploads, confirmation, and notification delivery.
- Verify no placeholder facts, fake testimonials, stock results, or exposed secrets remain.
- Run keyboard, reduced-motion, mobile camera-upload, and screen-reader checks.
- Verify structured data, Open Graph preview, canonical URL, robots, sitemap, and HTTPS.
- Confirm the current paid site stays online until the replacement is approved.
